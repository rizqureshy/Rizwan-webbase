Most enterprises treat responsible AI as a tax: a set of controls bolted on
after the model works. That framing is backwards. The organizations getting the
most durable value from AI are the ones that treat governance as part of the
product, not a gate at the end of it.

## The cost of getting this wrong

When trust breaks, adoption stalls. A single visible failure (a biased output, a
hallucinated number in a customer deal) can set a program back by quarters.

## What good looks like

- Clear ownership for every model in production.
- Transparency that a non technical stakeholder can actually read.
- Accountability that survives a reorg.

## A practical starting point

Pick one high visibility use case. Define what responsible means for that
specific case in one page. Ship it, measure it, and let that become the template
for the next ten.
