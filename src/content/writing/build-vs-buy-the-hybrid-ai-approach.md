The build versus buy debate assumes a binary that almost never exists in
practice. The interesting question is not which one, it is where to draw the
line so that the parts you buy accelerate the parts you build.

## Buy the commodity, build the differentiator

Foundation models, vector stores, and orchestration are increasingly commodity.
Your proprietary data, workflows, and judgment are not. Spend your scarce
engineering effort where it compounds.

## The hidden cost of building everything

Every component you build is a component you maintain forever. Be honest about
the total cost of ownership before you choose the satisfying path of building.

## A decision rule

If a capability is core to your differentiation and changes often, build it. If
it is undifferentiated and stable, buy it. Revisit the line every two quarters.
