A capable model that nobody uses creates zero value. The gap between a
successful pilot and a scaled rollout is rarely technical. It is human.

## Adoption is earned, not announced

People adopt tools that make their day easier and that they trust. Mandates
produce shadow workarounds. Usefulness produces pull.

## Guardrails create confidence

Counterintuitively, clear limits make people more willing to use AI, not less.
When the boundaries are explicit, the space inside them feels safe.

## Measure the behavior, not the launch

Track sustained usage and outcomes weeks after rollout, not signups on day one.
The real question is whether the work actually changed.
